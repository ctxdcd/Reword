THE IDEA BEHIND REWORD

Why REWORD?

REWORD began with a personal need: I wanted my own language-learning library with visual references and tools to help me remember vocabulary. I believe words are a good place to start — after all, how can we build sentences without knowing the words? Unable to afford expensive courses, I decided to build the learning space I wished I had.

I'm still learning to code, with AI helping along the way, but the ideas come from my own needs, and the 3D images are made by me. REWORD is free for personal use. For other licensing needs, please contact me first. This project is still a work in progress, so mistakes may happen. If you find it useful, optional donations are welcome.
